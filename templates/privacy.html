Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account we collect your name and email address. As you use Spendly, we store the expense information you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect basic technical information, such as your browser type, to keep the service working properly.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide and improve Spendly: to show your expenses, build spending summaries, keep your account secure, and respond to your requests. We do not sell your personal information or use your expense data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers and retained for as long as your account is active. We take reasonable steps to protect it from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You may request deletion of your account and data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly uses a small number of third-party services to operate, such as Google Fonts for typography. These providers may receive limited technical information, like your IP address, when you load the site. We do not share your expense data with them.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request access to or deletion of your data, please contact the Spendly team at the support address provided on our website.
